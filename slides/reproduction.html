R — Reproduction

  • Reproduction is how living things make offspring.
  • Asexual reproduction: one parent makes identical copies, like bacteria splitting in two.
  • Sexual reproduction: two parents each pass on half their DNA, so the offspring is unique.
  • Before a cell divides, it copies its DNA so each new cell gets a full set.
  • Cell division also helps our bodies grow and repair.

Key idea

Reproduction passes DNA from parents to offspring.

Reproduction is how living things make new living things, called offspring. There are two main types. In asexual reproduction, one parent makes identical copies of itself, like a bacterium splitting in two. In sexual reproduction, two parents each pass on half of their DNA, so the offspring is a unique mix of both. Point to the picture: before a cell divides, it copies its DNA, then it splits into two identical new cells. Our bodies use cell division to grow and to repair injuries.
