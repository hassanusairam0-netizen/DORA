D — DNA

  • DNA stands for Deoxyribonucleic Acid.
  • It carries the genetic instructions that help living things grow and function.
  • It holds information inherited from our parents.
  • It is shaped like a twisted ladder called a double helix.
  • In eukaryotic cells, most DNA is found in the nucleus.

Key idea

DNA is the instruction manual of a living organism.

DNA stands for deoxyribonucleic acid. It is like an instruction manual that tells living things how to grow and work. We inherit our DNA from our parents, which is why we can look like them. Point to the picture: DNA is shaped like a twisted ladder, called a double helix. The rungs of the ladder are pairs of bases. In cells like ours, most DNA is kept safely inside the nucleus.
